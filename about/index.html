About

Backends that keep the model in check.

I treat an LLM as one component of a backend, not the whole product. That means defining the contract first, putting guardrails around anything the model is allowed to do, handling the edge cases real users hit, and keeping the system explainable when it gets something wrong.

FinOps Agent’s autonomy gate is that idea in code: the model acts on its own only where it’s safe to, escalates when it isn’t sure, and is hard-blocked from the rest. The code is the proof, so open a repo rather than take my word for it.

I’m in my final year of a BSc in Computing Systems at Ulster University London, graduating in May 2027, and I work part-time at Aqua Shard alongside my studies.
