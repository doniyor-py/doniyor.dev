About

Backends that keep the model in check.

I treat an LLM as one component of a backend, not the whole product. That means defining the contract first, putting guardrails around anything the model is allowed to do, and handling the edge cases real users hit.

FinOps Agent’s autonomy gate is that idea in code: the model acts on its own only where it’s safe to, escalates when it isn’t sure, and is hard-blocked from the rest.

I’m in my final year of a BSc in Computing Systems at Ulster University London, graduating in May 2027.
